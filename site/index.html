Many forms of playing

Classic L0 — noise at the origin

trainwatch learning start

Fresh random nets, seeded. Generation 0 mostly stares at the ball. What to watch: the fitness strip — most nets die in seconds; the ring favors whoever survives longest.

Classic L1/L2 — committed champions

checkpointsbyte-reproducible

The repo ships level1 (gen 60) and level2 (gen 240), evolved by tools/prerun.js, md5-stable. Same seed → same bytes. What to watch: hits carry 100× fitness weight — the champion returns the ball, not just dodges it.

play

C1 · coevolution — a GAN pair of paddles

adversarialledger

Two populations: a survivor that must return the ball, an ender that wins by killing it. 121 receipted head-to-heads sealed in checkpoints/coev.js. What to watch: the ledger strip — every generation plotted, kills bright.
